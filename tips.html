Essential Guidelines for Detectives

  1. Observe the crime scene carefully before touching any physical evidence.
  2. Take detailed notes and record timestamps for every finding.
  3. Interview all available witnesses and verify their alibis.
  4. Analyze facts logically without jumping to premature conclusions.
  5. Maintain confidentiality and adhere to safety standards at all times.

Investigate Smart

A good detective relies on systematic analysis, sharp attention to detail, and persistent curiosity.
